Privacy

  • What you type or upload is sent to the MediAI server, and from there to Google's Gemini API to create the answer.
  • MediAI does not save your text, files or answers on its server. Uploaded files are handled in memory only.
  • If the site uses a free-tier Gemini key, Google's terms may allow your content to be used to improve its products. For demos, use sample data, not real personal medical records.
  • Your language choice is remembered in your browser. The Health Summary form is saved on your device only if you tick the save box, and you can clear it at any time.
  • MediAI is not a medical record system and does not keep one.
